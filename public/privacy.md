# Privacy Policy — Physics Wallah — EduVibe

> Last Updated: October 16, 2026 • Effective Immediately

## 1. Introduction
EduVibe ("we", "our", or "us") operates **https://pw.eduvibe.cc.cd** (the "Service"), providing students across India with open, distraction-free access to Physics Wallah (PW) educational courses, video lectures, practice quizzes, and study resources.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you access or use our Physics Wallah educational portal, Progressive Web Application (PWA), and integrated authentication services.

EduVibe is a **100% free, non-commercial public educational initiative**. We never solicit fees, process billing or financial transactions, or monetize study materials.

By accessing or using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any terms herein, please discontinue using the Service.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect limited, necessary information to deliver an optimal learning, progress-tracking, and navigation experience:
- **Google Account Data (OAuth 2.0)**: When you choose to sign in using Google Identity Services, we may collect your Google account name, email address, and profile photo URL. We only request basic profile and email scopes required to authenticate your identity.
- **Academic Progress Data**: Information about your watched lectures, saved video positions, completed Daily Practice Problems (DPPs), practice test scores, study streaks, and earned XP.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data**: Technical metadata including browser type, operating system, language preferences, IP address, and standard diagnostic/error logs to maintain server stability.
- **Local Storage & Browser Cache**: We use browser `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` to store playback preferences, theme settings, offline bookmarks, and local progress buffers.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information strictly for legitimate educational and operational purposes:
- To authenticate your identity and personalize your student dashboard.
- To maintain your lecture watch positions so you can resume videos where you left off.
- To compute daily study streaks (requiring 20 XP per day) and 14-day league leaderboard ranks.
- To prevent abuse, automated scraping, and cheating via our capped XP rate-limiting engine.
- To monitor, secure, and improve the performance and edge delivery of our web platform.

## 4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure
EduVibe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your Google user data** to any third parties, advertisers, or commercial data brokers.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information
We respect student privacy and do not disclose personal information to third parties except under the following strict conditions:
- **Infrastructure Providers**: Secure hosting, database, and edge CDN providers (e.g., Cloudflare, Render, Supabase) that assist in operating the web application under confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Requirements**: If required to comply with a valid legal obligation, court order, or governmental inquiry.
- **Platform Protection**: To protect the rights, property, and safety of EduVibe, our learners, and the public.

## 6. Data Security & Retention
We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) to protect data transmitted between your browser and our servers. User authentication tokens are securely handled via modern JWT standards. We retain personal and progress data only for as long as necessary to provide educational functionality.

## 7. Your Rights and Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your personal data:
- **Google Permissions**: You can revoke EduVibe's access at any time via [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **Local Cache**: You can clear all stored progress, themes, and offline data by clearing your browser cache and cookies.
- **Account Deletion**: You may request the permanent removal of your account and progress data by contacting our support team at **privacy@eduvibe.cc.cd**.

## 8. Children’s Privacy
EduVibe is designed as an educational platform for students preparing for high school boards and competitive entrance exams. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental or institutional guidance.
