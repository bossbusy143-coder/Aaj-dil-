## Flagship Batches Directory

### 1. Arjuna Batch (Class 11)
- **Target Audience**: Students transitioning from Class 10 to Class 11.
- **Streams**: JEE (Main & Advanced) and NEET-UG.
- **Focus**: Building rock-solid fundamentals in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology from scratch, bridging the critical gap between school boards and competitive entrance exams.

### 2. Lakshya Batch (Class 12)
- **Target Audience**: Students studying in Class 12.
- **Streams**: JEE (Main & Advanced) and NEET-UG + Board Exams.
- **Focus**: Full coverage of the Class 12 board curriculum in synchronization with high-yield competitive entrance preparation, followed by extensive Class 11 revision marathons and mock test drills.

### 3. Yakeen Batch (NEET Droppers / Repeaters)
- **Target Audience**: High-intensity gap-year preparation for medical aspirants.
- **Focus**: Targeted score-boosting, rapid backlog clearing, NCERT line-by-line Biology mastery, physical chemistry numerical drills, and rigorous All-India test series.

### 4. Prayas Batch (JEE Droppers / Repeaters)
- **Target Audience**: Gap-year repeaters targeting JEE Main & Advanced.
- **Focus**: Advanced multi-concept problem decomposition, high-speed problem-solving techniques, previous-year question (PYQ) dissection, and IIT top-rank pedagogy.

### 5. Foundation & Board Batches (Classes 9–10)
- **Streams**: Udaan (Class 10), Neev (Class 9).
- **Focus**: NCERT conceptual depth, Olympiad readiness, board exam sample papers, and foundation building for future JEE/NEET aspirants.

## Platform Features & Learning Tools

- **Comprehensive Batches Directory (`/batches`)**: Explore live and recorded lectures organized cleanly by exam stream, subject, chapter, and topic.
- **High-Performance Video Player (`/video`)**: Integrated player with adaptive streaming, variable playback speed (0.5x–3.0x), timestamp notes, video download options, and auto-resume from last saved position.
- **Daily Practice Problems (`/practice`)**: Chapter-wise DPP quizzes with instant scoring, step-by-step explanations, and video solutions.
- **Infinite Adaptive Practice (`/infinite-practice`)**: Dynamic question generator allowing limitless practice drills tailored to chosen difficulty levels and specific subtopics.
- **Khazana Lecture Archive (`/study/khazana`)**: Instant access to legendary masterclasses and historical lectures from India's most celebrated PW educators.
- **Digital Library & PDF Viewer (`/library`, `/study-material`)**: High-yield revision formula sheets, handwritten class notes, theory modules, and integrated in-app PDF reading.
- **Tracker360 & Analytics (`/tracker`)**: Real-time visualization of syllabus completion percentage, daily lecture hours, subject-wise progress meters, and study streaks.
- **Gamified Study Streaks & Leaderboard (`/profile`, `/leaderboard`)**: Daily study habit reinforcement requiring 20 XP/day to maintain consecutive streaks, competing in 30-participant 14-day cycle leagues.
- **Schedule & Study Planner (`/schedule`)**: Daily class timetables, upcoming live streams, and structured revision timelines.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux for instant home-screen launching and distraction-free learning.
